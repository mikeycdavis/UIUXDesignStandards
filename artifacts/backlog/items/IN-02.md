## What

The four deliberately deferred bodies of work: version-identity hardening and the reusable workflow,
a real-project dogfood, a browser-evidence producer, and portfolio integration.

## Why

Each of these needs something v1.0.0 does not have — an immutable ref to pin, an owner-nominated
target repository, a dependency policy that permits a browser, or sibling repositories to wire into.

## Notes

Recorded, not dropped. Plan sections 13 through 16 exist and are authored; the deferral is visible
rather than being silent scope loss.
