## What

Plan section 00. The owner's prompts and decisions recorded verbatim as repository artifacts, twelve
ADRs, and the seventeen-file plan breakdown.

## Why

Repository artifacts are canonical over chat. A decision that exists only in a conversation cannot be
cited, checked, or contradicted later.

## Notes

Every owner decision carries `CONFIRMED_BY_OWNER` with a date, because an undated owner confirmation
is itself a finding under the epistemic-labelling rules.
