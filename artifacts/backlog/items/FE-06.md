## What

Plan section 05. Evidence-based UI applicability: APPLICABLE, NOT_APPLICABLE, or INDETERMINATE, with
the signal families, the scan-completeness record, and the three-block output envelope.

## Why

Applicability is never a rule result, and it is never established by self-declaration alone.

## Notes

Classifier uncertainty and classifier non-execution are mechanically separate: INDETERMINATE is a
classification and exits 0, while a classifier that could not run exits 2 and emits no classification
at all.
