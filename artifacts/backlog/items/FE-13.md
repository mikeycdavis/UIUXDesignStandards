## What

Plan section 12. A release-readiness checker that aggregates existing evidence, a frozen catalog
snapshot treated as an independent oracle, `VERSION`, `CHANGELOG.md`, the readiness report, and
finally the `v1.0.0` tag.

## Why

Readiness has to be consumed from evidence rather than manufactured from exit codes. A checker that
concludes "ready" because every command it happened to run exited 0 has re-created the false green at
the release layer.

## Notes

Three release states stay distinct: IMPLEMENTED means the artifact exists, VERIFIED means the
acceptance claim was demonstrated, RELEASED means an immutable tag exists. Before the tag the report
legitimately reads `implementation: VERIFIED` and `release: NOT_RELEASED`, and the checker still
exits 0 — requiring the future tag in order to say the tag may be created would be circular.

The checker found two things nobody was looking for. Two forbidden rules —
`design-integrity.no-fake-progress` and `accessibility.no-inaccessible-custom-controls` — were typed
`code-analysis` with no detector, which the plan had named in advance as unacceptable. They were first
recorded as release gaps; that was rejected on review and both were **resolved** instead under ADR
0014, because a gap is only honest where the evidence cannot now be recovered, and these were
fixable. The second finding was a hole in the snapshot comparison: it checked the recorded digest
against the live catalog but never against the recorded rules, so an edited snapshot could compare
clean while describing a catalog that never existed.

Readiness now separates the two kinds of gap mechanically. `GAP_POLICY` lists the criteria permitted
to carry an accepted limitation; anything else reporting a gap is `BLOCKING_GAP` and the release is
`NOT_READY` however accurately it was written down.

v1.0.0 was tagged at `8353469` and published to `origin` on 2026-08-11, then verified from a clone of
the published tag rather than from the authoring working tree — which is what found the line-ending
and chronology-anchor defects recorded in plan section 12.

The chronology gap is permanently `NOT_ESTABLISHED` for this development history and was not
manufactured retroactively.
