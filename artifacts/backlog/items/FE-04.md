## What

Plan section 03. The frozen identity artifact written before any detector code, then the fifteen rule
files it authorises: 70 rules, 15 of them forbidden.

## Why

If detectors can influence identity, the evaluator has quietly become the catalog.

## Notes

The freeze's original verification was a git-history ordering check, which is not runnable while
nothing is committed — it would pass over two empty sets. It is recorded as deferred, guarded by a
watchdog test that upgrades itself to the real comparison the moment history exists, and the ordering
is enforced meanwhile by content through `scripts/rule-identity.mjs`.
