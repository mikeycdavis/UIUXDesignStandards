## What

Plan sections 00 through 03: the process bootstrap, the repository skeleton and vendored core, the
forty standards with their external provenance, and the frozen rule catalog.

## Why

Rule identity has to be frozen before any detector exists, or the evaluator starts defining what it
is evaluating.

## Notes

The corpus, not the plan, determined the count. The plan estimated ~55 rules; the forty standards
name seventy identities. The freeze asserts 70 rules / 15 forbidden / 13 detectors, and those numbers
are consumed downstream rather than recomputed.
