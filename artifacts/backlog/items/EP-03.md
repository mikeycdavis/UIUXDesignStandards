## What

Plan sections 07 through 09: the browser-evidence ingestion contract, the manual-review and
attestation model, and the `init` bootstrap.

## Why

Most UX properties are not statically decidable. Each additional surface has to obey the same
epistemic discipline as the static one, or it becomes the soft place where claims get established
without evidence.

## Notes

Four independent evidence axes — did the run complete, is it fresh, did it cover the declared
subject, what did the check say — and attestations add a fifth: was the review scope declared by
something other than the reviewer.
