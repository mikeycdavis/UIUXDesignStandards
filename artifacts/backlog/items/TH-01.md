## What

A standalone, versioned, machine-readable UI/UX/design standards framework: a normative corpus, a
rule catalog that owns rule identity, a project policy that owns applicability, and an evaluator that
produces evidence.

## Why

UI and design quality is the surface where "we could not check it" most easily becomes "it passed".
This theme exists to make that conversion impossible: the framework must never convert an inability
to evaluate into a pass.

## Notes

The governing law, restated in every milestone: the catalog defines rule identity and metadata, the
project policy defines project applicability, the evaluator produces evidence, and none of the three
may redefine the others.
