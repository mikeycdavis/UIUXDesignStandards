## What

Plan section 02. Forty numbered standards, the source inventory that extraction is tested against,
and the external-source provenance record with its checker.

## Why

Citing WCAG or a platform HIG has to justify a rule's origin without becoming a second rule
authority. The local normative text stays the only authority.

## Notes

A provenance mapping carries no normative fields by schema construction, so an external source cannot
redefine a local rule even by accident.
