## What

Plan section 04. The policy schema, the cross-field semantic invariants that the schema vocabulary
cannot express, the adoption templates, and this repository's own policy.

## Why

A malformed policy is a configuration error, not a failing project. Conflating the two teaches a
consumer that a broken config is a compliance problem they can except their way out of.

## Notes

The vendored JSON-Schema evaluator has a closed keyword set and was deliberately not extended to fake
conditionality. The six cross-field invariants live in `policy.mjs` and fail at exit 2.
