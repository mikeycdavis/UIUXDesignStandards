## What

Plan sections 10 through 12: the invariant registry and falsifier harness, CI and the documentation,
and release readiness through the v1.0.0 tag.

## Why

A green suite is not evidence unless it has been shown able to go red. This epic is where the
framework's own promises stop being asserted and start being defended.

## Notes

The falsifier harness caught its own false green on first run: a `node --test` child inheriting
NODE_TEST_CONTEXT ran nothing and exited 0, so every mutation reported as uncaught.
