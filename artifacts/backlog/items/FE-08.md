## What

Plan section 07. The evidence document schema, its ingestion and verification, freshness against
committed content, and the always-present evidence-surface block.

## Why

The framework defines and verifies the evidence contract; it never produces browser evidence, because
bundling a browser would end the zero-dependency law.

## Notes

Until a producer exists, every browser-analysis rule reports not-evaluated. That cost is the point: a
visible gap is the correct output, and faking the surface would be the failure this repository exists
to prevent.
