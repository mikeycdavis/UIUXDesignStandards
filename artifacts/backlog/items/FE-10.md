## What

Plan section 09. A pure planner and a single writer, three modes, and epistemic labels preserved
through bootstrap.

## Why

The risk in a bootstrapper is not false compliance; it is false history. Scaffolding must never read
as a record of what a project decided.

## Notes

`init` may never scaffold `no-ui`. That claim needs a complete scan with zero signals; a wrong
`web-ui` guess produces a visible INDETERMINATE, whereas a wrong `no-ui` would produce a silent
exemption of the entire UI rule surface.
