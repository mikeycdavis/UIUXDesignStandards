## What

Plan section 01. `package.json` with no dependencies, the vendored YAML and JSON-Schema evaluators,
the adapted catalog and compliance modules, and the shared content-identity primitive.

## Why

Freshness claims need exactly one owner. Two implementations of the same digest drift, and the drift
is invisible until it produces a false FRESH.

## Notes

The parent framework's working-tree-content digest is a known defect and was deliberately not ported:
identical commits materialise different bytes across clean checkouts. Identity resolves from the
committed tree at a recorded revision, never from the index and never from working-tree bytes.
