## What

Plan sections 04 through 06: the policy schema and its cross-field semantics, the Gate 1
applicability classifier, and the evaluator with its thirteen static detectors.

## Why

These are the three gates. Gate 0 establishes that the policy is valid configuration, Gate 1 whether
a UI is applicable at all, and Gate 2 whether the applicable UI satisfies the standards.

## Notes

Failure to establish applicability is INDETERMINATE, never NOT_APPLICABLE. NOT_APPLICABLE is
deliberately the hardest state to obtain, because it is the state that exempts the entire UI rule
surface.
