## What

Plan section 08. The attestation model with an explicit nine-step precedence table, path-scoped
freshness, and a declared review subject the reviewer does not choose.

## Why

Human assertion has to obey the same discipline as machine evidence, or it becomes the way to
establish anything without evidence.

## Notes

Evidence outranks assertion: a witnessed failure contradicts a fresh approved review. Three separate
questions are kept separate — was the review fresh, was its subject declared, did it cover that
declared subject.
